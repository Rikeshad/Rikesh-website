02 // ABOUT

ABOUT
MY WORK.

My work centres on telecommunications network planning, supported by desktop software and web development. I enjoy understanding how information moves through a system and turning that understanding into clear, useful interfaces.

Originally from Nepal, I bring curiosity about people and places to the systems I build. This portfolio shares that ongoing work through real interfaces and project decisions.

I LOVE DARWIN.

Darwin feels like home to me. I value the community, the tropical lifestyle and the character of the Northern Territory. I want to stay connected to Darwin for the long term by living, working and contributing here.
