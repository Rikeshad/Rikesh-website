02 / PROJECT

BrewBean

A modern e-commerce coffee store created using WordPress and WooCommerce, designed to provide customers with a simple, attractive and responsive online shopping experience.

01 / ABOUT

About the Project

BrewBean is an online coffee store developed to demonstrate the design and development of a complete e-commerce website. The project focuses on product presentation, customer usability, responsive design and online store functionality.

02 / FEATURES

Key Features

Online Store

Customers can browse coffee products through a structured and easy-to-use online storefront.

Product Catalogue

Products are organised with information such as names, descriptions, prices and product images.

Shopping Cart

Customers can add products to their cart and manage selected items before checkout.

Customer Accounts

Customers can register, sign in and manage their account information through the website.

Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Secure Website

HTTPS and SSL are used to provide a secure connection between visitors and the website.

03 / TECHNOLOGY

Technology Used

BrewBean was developed using WordPress and WooCommerce with supporting web technologies for design and customisation.

WordPress WooCommerce HTML CSS JavaScript
04 / PURPOSE

Project Goal

The goal of BrewBean is to create a professional and user-friendly e-commerce experience where customers can discover coffee products, view product information and purchase products through an organised online store.
